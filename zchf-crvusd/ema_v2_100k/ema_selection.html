Interpretation

While slower EMAs reduce modeled losses for borrowers, they also make the oracle less responsive to changes in market prices. The ZCHF/crvUSD Curve pool currently uses an EMA half-life of 3,603 seconds (approximately 1 hour). For now, this appears to provide a reasonable balance between oracle responsiveness and borrower-loss reduction. Accordingly, a 60-minute EMA will be used for the remaining parameterization.
